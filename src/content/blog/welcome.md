Welcome! If you are caring for someone you love who is living with dementia or Alzheimer's, you are in the right place.

Caregiving can be beautiful and exhausting on the same afternoon. Some days you will find yourself laughing with your loved one over a burnt batch of cookies. Other days you will wonder how to explain to your children why Grandma doesn't remember their name. You don't have to figure it all out alone.

Here is what you'll find on this site as it grows:

- **Encouragement and practical tips** for the everyday moments of caregiving.
- **The adventures of Grandma Rose and Lily**, gentle stories that help families talk with children about memory loss.
- **A list of caregiver resources**, from national helplines to local support near you.
- **Activities** designed to engage your loved one at different stages of the disease (coming soon).

Thank you for being here. Take a deep breath. You are doing better than you think.
